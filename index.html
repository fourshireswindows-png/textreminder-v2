Common questions

Does it work for regular window-cleaning rounds?
Yes. Add recurring jobs and TextReminder sends customers a message the evening before or the morning of their clean, alongside your existing round management.

Do I need to replace my existing diary or round software?
No. TextReminder works alongside whatever you already use — a paper diary, Google Calendar, Cleaner Planner, Squeegee or a spreadsheet. It's the communication layer, not a replacement.
