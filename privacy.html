Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Who we are

TextReminder is an SMS appointment reminder service for UK tradespeople, operated by Hey Monkey Ltd. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at hello@textreminder.co.uk.

2. What data we collect

We collect the following data when you use TextReminder:

Account data: your email address and password (stored securely via Supabase Auth).

Google Calendar data: when you connect your Google Calendar, we access your calendar events (titles, dates, times, locations) to schedule reminders. We do not store your Google credentials — only a refresh token to maintain the connection.

Customer phone numbers: phone numbers you add to calendar events so we can send SMS reminders to your customers.

Business details: your business name and phone number, used to personalise reminder messages.

Message logs: records of SMS reminders sent, including status and timestamp.

3. How we use your data

We use your data solely to provide the TextReminder service: sending SMS appointment reminders to your customers on your behalf. We do not sell, share, or use your data for advertising or marketing purposes.

4. SMS and customer data

By using TextReminder, you confirm that your customers have consented to receive SMS reminders from you. You are responsible for ensuring your use of the service complies with UK GDPR and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR).

All SMS messages include an opt-out instruction ("Reply STOP to opt out"). We process opt-outs on your behalf where technically possible.

5. Data storage and security

All data is stored within the UK and EU on Supabase infrastructure. We use industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest. Access to your data is protected by row-level security — only you can access your own data.

6. Google OAuth

TextReminder's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

We use Google Calendar read access only. We do not modify, delete, or share your Google Calendar data. You can revoke access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

7. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated data is permanently deleted within 30 days. You can request deletion at any time by emailing hello@textreminder.co.uk.

8. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data; to object to or restrict processing; and to data portability. To exercise any of these rights, contact hello@textreminder.co.uk.

9. Cookies

TextReminder uses only essential cookies required for authentication (session tokens). We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be notified by email. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

For privacy-related enquiries: hello@textreminder.co.uk
